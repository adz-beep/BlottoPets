Playing Blotto Pets

You do not need an account to play. This release does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or a developer-operated server that receives your gameplay information.

Information on your device

Solo game information is stored locally on your device. Device backups may include app data, depending on your Apple settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data from that device; backups are managed separately in your Apple settings.

Playing with someone nearby

If you choose nearby multiplayer, Blotto Pets uses Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity framework to discover and connect with another player. Your device’s display name may be visible to nearby players. Connected devices exchange the game information needed to play the match.

Connections use encryption provided by Apple’s framework. This information is not sent to a server operated by us.

The app may request local-network access for nearby multiplayer. You can manage this permission in your device’s Settings. Solo and pass-and-play do not require a nearby connection.
